Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

In short: Inkzy does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. The game works offline, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or tracking.

This policy explains how the mobile game Inkzy ("the app") handles information. The app is developed and published by Hayk Manucharyan, trading as thehayk ("I", "me"). It applies to the app on iOS, iPadOS and Android.

1. Information I collect

None. Inkzy does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information, and it does not send any information to me or to anyone else.

2. Information stored on your device

To save your game, Inkzy keeps a small database locally on your device only. It contains:

  • your progress on each level: completed levels, best steps and times, stars, and a level you left unfinished;
  • your settings: sound, music and their volumes, timer and hints.

This data never leaves your device. I cannot see it. You can erase it with Settings → Reset progress, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

3. Network access, advertising and analytics

Inkzy does not need or use an internet connection. It contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, no social-media features, and no third-party software development kits that collect data. There are no in-app purchases and no accounts.

4. Sharing of information

Because I collect no information, I do not sell, rent, trade or share any information with third parties.

5. App stores

You download Inkzy from the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google may collect information about your download and device under their own privacy policies, which I do not control. See Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

6. Children's privacy

Inkzy does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because the app collects no personal information at all, it can be played without sharing any data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Since I hold no personal data about Inkzy players, there is nothing to access or delete; your locally saved progress and settings can be removed with Reset progress or by deleting the app. You can still contact me with any privacy question.

8. Security

The only data the app keeps stays inside the app's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, I will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.

10. Contact
