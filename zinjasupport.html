How to play

Jump
Tap the right half of the screen.
Throw a shuriken
Tap the left half of the screen. You start each run with 3; a golden shuriken gives 5 more.
Defeat a ghoul
Land on its head or hit it with a shuriken. Touch it any other way and the run is over.
Speed
The world gets faster every 10 kills.

Frequently asked questions

Does Zinja need an internet connection?
No. The game works fully offline.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No.
Where is my best score saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your best score.
How do I turn off music or sound?
Use the music and sound buttons in the top-left corner of the main menu.
