How to play

Move the pen
Tap a neighbouring tile or swipe diagonally. Every tile the pen leaves turns to ink.
Swap
Turns every parchment tile into ink and every ink tile into parchment. It counts as one step.
Win
Ink every parchment tile, then finish on the ink monster.
Stars
Beat the level's par number of steps for three stars.
The Ink Flood
Level 51 unlocks after level 50: a real-time hunt for all 50 monsters.

Frequently asked questions

Does Inkzy need an internet connection?
No. The game works fully offline.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No.
Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.
How do I start over?
Open Settings and choose Reset progress.
Can I turn off the timer, music or sounds?
Yes, in Settings: Show timer, Music, Sound effects and their volumes.
